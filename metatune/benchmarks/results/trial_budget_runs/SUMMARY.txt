Trial-budget experiment: does crossing the 50-trial Thompson-sampling threshold
(see CHANGELOG.md, "Testing the trial-budget hypothesis") change the picture?
seed 42 unless noted. Raw JSONs in this folder.

digits_svm (accuracy, maximize) -- already saturated, uninformative:
  30 trials:  ours=0.9889  optuna=0.9889
  60 trials:  ours=0.9889  optuna=0.9889
  100 trials: ours=0.9889  optuna=0.9889

synthetic_xgboost_gmm (rmse, minimize) -- gap narrows steadily:
  30 trials:  ours=53.2476  optuna=52.9923  (optuna +0.48%)
  60 trials:  ours=53.2387  optuna=52.9923  (optuna +0.47%, optuna flat)
  100 trials: ours=53.0056  optuna=52.9923  (optuna +0.03%, nearly closed)

diabetes_xgboost (rmse, minimize) -- seed 42 only, ours wins:
  30 trials:  ours=52.4405  optuna=51.7021  (optuna +1.4%)
  60 trials:  ours=51.4243  optuna=51.7021  (ours +0.5%)
  100 trials: ours=51.6180  optuna=51.6321  (ours +0.03%)

  Checked at 60 trials on seeds 43 and 44 before trusting the seed-42 win:
  seed 43: ours=55.2139  optuna=53.5846  (optuna wins; ours also got WORSE
           than its own 30-trial result of 54.5359 -- a real regression, not
           just "didn't improve enough")
  seed 44: ours=52.5056  optuna=52.2333  (optuna wins narrowly)
  -> 1 ours win / 2 optuna wins across 3 seeds at 60 trials. The seed-42 win
     does not generalize as a reliable pattern.

imbalanced_pipeline (f1, maximize) -- catches up to an exact tie:
  30 trials:  ours=0.4100  optuna=0.4149  (optuna +1.2%)
  60 trials:  ours=0.4149  optuna=0.4211  (optuna +1.5%, both improved)
  100 trials: ours=0.4211  optuna=0.4211  (tie)

Conclusion: real, structural reason (confirmed in code) why the 30-trial
protocol underrates `ours` -- it never reaches the optimizer's strongest
strategies. Real but seed-dependent evidence that more trials helps. Not
strong enough to overturn "Optuna wins most comparisons in this session";
strong enough to caveat all of them.
